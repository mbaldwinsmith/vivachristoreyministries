About Roisin

A life shaped by encounter, prayer and mission.

Roisin Doherty is a Catholic lay minister whose years of service have grown through prayer ministry, evangelisation, healing nights, pilgrimage, formation and collaboration with priests, deacons and religious communities.

Her ministry is centred on helping people encounter Jesus Christ personally, receive prayer with dignity and hope, and discover the freedom and joy of life in the Holy Spirit.

From street ministry in Derry to national conferences, pilgrimage teams and small-group formation, the thread has remained the same: make room for God to act, keep Christ at the centre, and serve people with love.

Formation & training

Charismatic ministry with deep roots.

Roisin is an Encounter Ministries graduate, completing Years 1, 2 and 3, and serving as an online small-group leader during Year 3.

Her formation has been lived as well as studied: in prayer teams, parish and conference settings, pilgrimage, street evangelisation, healing ministry and the formation of other lay ministers.

Invite Roisin

Let’s discern what would serve your community.

Roisin is open to invitations for prayer ministry, speaking, retreats, workshops, pilgrimage and evangelisation events.

Every invitation begins with conversation and discernment. Practical and pastoral arrangements are agreed with hosts in advance, including clergy oversight where appropriate, suitable facilities for prayer ministry, safeguarding, travel and accommodation, event expectations and costs.

Start a conversation

A good invitation includes

  • 01 The purpose and audience of the event
  • 02 Proposed dates and location
  • 03 Parish, priest or ministry-team context where relevant
  • 04 Prayer-ministry space and facilities
  • 05 Travel, accommodation and financial arrangements
  • 06 Safeguarding and local pastoral responsibility
